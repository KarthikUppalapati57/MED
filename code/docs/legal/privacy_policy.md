# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 24, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 24, 2026

Mindful Tech Solutions Inc., doing business as RestOps-360 (“RestOps-360,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), provides a restaurant-operations software platform for invoice capture, accounts-payable workflows, vendor management, inventory, recipes, reporting, payments, integrations, and AI-assisted operational tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit `https://restops-360.com`, use the RestOps-360 platform, communicate with us, or receive text messages from us.

## 1. Scope and Roles

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- visitors to our websites and public pages;
- customer administrators and authorized users of the RestOps-360 platform;
- restaurant personnel, supplier representatives, vendor contacts, and other individuals whose information is submitted to the platform; and
- individuals who request authentication or account-security text messages.

For personal information a business customer submits to the platform, the customer generally determines why and how the information is processed, and RestOps-360 acts as its service provider or processor. Requests concerning customer-controlled information may need to be directed to the applicable customer organization.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, services, or integrations that have their own privacy notices.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account and identity information

Name, business email address, mobile number, user identifier, role, organization, brand, location assignment, invitation status, authentication records, multi-factor authentication status, notification settings, and electronic acceptance records.

### 2.2 Restaurant and business-operational information

Organizations, restaurant locations, suppliers, vendor contacts, invoices, invoice line items, purchase and approval records, products, inventory counts, recipes, labor information, operational reports, point-of-sale and delivery-platform data, accounting references, webhooks, integration metadata, and API configuration data.

### 2.3 Vendor, tax, banking, and payment information

Where enabled and authorized, information may include business names, contact information, tax classification, EIN or SSN, W-9 documents, bank account and routing information, ACH authorizations, electronic signatures, payment status, payout information, transaction references, and limited display information such as the last four digits of an account.

Payment providers may collect or process payment information directly under their own terms and privacy notices.

### 2.4 Device, usage, and security information

IP address, browser and device information, user agent, timestamps, session and login metadata, tenant and location routing information, audit events, application logs, error information, access-control decisions, and security-related activity.

### 2.5 Communications and support information

Information you provide in support requests, emails, calls, forms, feedback, surveys, demonstrations, and other communications with us.

### 2.6 SMS and authentication information

When you request a verification code, we may process your mobile number, the date and time of your request, consent and disclosure records, delivery status, opt-out status, carrier or routing metadata, and security-event information associated with the authentication flow.

Our launch SMS program is limited to user-requested, transactional security messages for registration, login, phone verification, multi-factor authentication, and account recovery. We do not use consent to this security-message program to send marketing texts.

## 3. Sources of Personal Information

We collect information:

- directly from you;
- from the customer organization that creates or administers your account;
- from vendors or supplier contacts entered by an authorized customer;
- from integrations that the customer authorizes, such as point-of-sale, accounting, payment, banking, or delivery services;
- automatically from use of the platform; and
- from service providers that help us authenticate users, host the service, process payments, send communications, secure the platform, or provide support.

## 4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

- provide, configure, maintain, and support the platform;
- authenticate users and deliver requested one-time passcodes;
- administer organizations, brands, locations, roles, and permissions;
- process invoices, approvals, inventory, recipes, vendor records, payments, and reports;
- operate customer-authorized integrations;
- send service, security, billing, and support communications;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
- maintain audit trails and business records;
- improve reliability, usability, and performance;
- enforce agreements and acceptable-use requirements;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, financial, payment-network, and regulatory obligations; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use mobile opt-in information or text-message consent for third-party or affiliate marketing.

## 5. AI-Assisted Features

Approved production AI features may use Microsoft Azure OpenAI to assist with invoice extraction, document processing, chatbot functions, or operational analysis. Customer information is not submitted by us for training public or shared foundation models.

AI-generated or AI-extracted information may be incomplete or inaccurate. Authorized users must review material outputs, including extracted invoice data, before relying on them for approvals, accounting, payment, inventory, purchasing, or other operational decisions.

## 6. How We Disclose Personal Information

We may disclose personal information:

- to the customer organization that controls the applicable account;
- to authorized users and administrators within the customer’s configured tenant, brand, and location scope;
- to service providers and subprocessors that host, secure, support, or deliver the platform;
- to payment, banking, and payout providers when a customer enables those services;
- to professional advisers, auditors, insurers, and financing parties subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations;
- in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction;
- when required by law, subpoena, court order, or lawful government request; or
- when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, customers, users, the public, or the platform.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

## 8. SMS Privacy Commitments

When you request a security code:

- we send messages only after you enter your number and affirmatively select the applicable send or verification action;
- message frequency varies based on the number of authentication requests you initiate;
- message and data rates may apply;
- you may reply **STOP** to opt out and **HELP** for help;
- opting out may prevent use of SMS-based verification until you re-enroll or use another available authentication method; and
- consent is not a condition of purchasing a subscription, although phone verification may be required for a specific security workflow.

We may share information needed to deliver messages with Microsoft Azure Communication Services, telecommunications carriers, aggregators, and related delivery providers. Mobile information and text-message consent are not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

See the separate [SMS Terms and Conditions](sms_terms_and_conditions.md).

## 9. Cookies and Local Storage

We currently use browser storage that is necessary to authenticate users, maintain sessions, support security workflows, remember functional preferences, and operate the web application. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or production analytics cookies.

See the [Cookie Policy](cookie_policy.md) for more information.

## 10. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the service, maintain security and audit records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention depends on the type of information and context. For example:

- account and customer data is generally retained during the subscription and for a limited post-termination export period;
- archived customer data may be retained for up to 30 days before deletion, unless a longer period is required by law or agreed in writing;
- tax, payment, ACH, electronic-signature, accounting, and transaction records may be retained for legally required recordkeeping periods;
- security logs may be retained for a period appropriate to fraud prevention, incident investigation, and legal obligations; and
- SMS consent and opt-out records may be retained for at least four years or longer when reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance.

Backups may retain information for a limited additional period before being overwritten through normal backup cycles.

## 11. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. Controls may include role-based access, tenant and location scoping, database row-level security, encryption in transit, infrastructure-provider encryption at rest, restricted secrets handling, audit logging, session controls, multi-factor authentication options, vulnerability management, and incident-response procedures.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting credentials, configuring authorized users appropriately, and notifying us promptly of suspected unauthorized access.

See our [Security Overview](security_policy.md).

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

- request access to personal information;
- request correction of inaccurate information;
- request deletion;
- obtain a portable copy of certain information;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- appeal a denial of a privacy request.

We do not discriminate against individuals for exercising applicable privacy rights.

To submit a request, email `contact@mindfultechsol.com` with the subject line **Privacy Request**. We may verify your identity and authority before completing a request. Authorized agents may be required to provide proof of authorization. When information is controlled by a customer organization, we may direct the request to that organization or assist it in responding.

Do not send full Social Security numbers, tax documents, passwords, or banking credentials by ordinary email.
