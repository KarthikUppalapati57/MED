# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** July 24, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 24, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) applies to access to and use of RestOps-360 and is incorporated into the [Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in the Terms.

## 1. General Rule

You may use the Services only for lawful, authorized business purposes and in a manner that does not harm the Services, other customers, third parties, telecommunications networks, payment networks, or the public.

You are responsible for violations by users, contractors, integrations, systems, and applications operating through your account.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You must not, and must not enable or assist anyone to:

### 2.1 Security and access abuse

- access or attempt to access data outside an authorized tenant, organization, brand, location, role, or account;
- bypass authentication, multi-factor authentication, authorization, row-level security, rate limits, usage limits, payment controls, or other safeguards;
- probe, scan, penetration test, or attempt to discover vulnerabilities without our prior written authorization;
- introduce malware, ransomware, malicious code, corrupted data, or harmful instructions;
- use compromised credentials, share credentials improperly, or impersonate another person or organization;
- interfere with, overload, disrupt, or degrade the Services or another customer’s use;
- extract secrets, source code, private keys, model internals, training data, or non-public system information; or
- attempt to reverse engineer or create a competing service from protected elements of the Services, except where a restriction is prohibited by law.

### 2.2 Data and privacy abuse

- submit personal information without an appropriate legal basis, notice, authority, or consent;
- upload content you do not have the right to use;
- expose full tax identifiers, banking credentials, passwords, security codes, or other sensitive information through inappropriate fields or communications;
- scrape, harvest, enrich, profile, or sell personal information in violation of law or contractual restrictions;
- defeat retention, deletion, access-control, or audit requirements; or
- use the Services to stalk, harass, discriminate against, or unlawfully monitor an individual.

### 2.3 Payments, vendor, and financial abuse

- submit false, stolen, or unauthorized tax, banking, vendor, payout, invoice, or payment information;
- change payout or bank information without required verification and authorization;
- initiate or approve fraudulent, deceptive, duplicative, unauthorized, or unlawful transactions;
- misuse ACH, card, payout, refund, chargeback, or payment-retry functionality;
- falsify invoices, approvals, W-9 records, electronic signatures, audit events, or supporting documents; or
- use payment features in violation of provider terms, NACHA rules, sanctions, anti-money-laundering requirements, or applicable law.

### 2.4 Messaging and communications abuse

- send messages without legally sufficient and documented consent;
- send unsolicited, deceptive, misleading, fraudulent, harassing, or unlawful messages;
- use purchased, rented, scraped, transferred, or shared consent lists;
- send message types, brands, or campaigns different from what the recipient agreed to receive;
- ignore, delay, evade, or circumvent STOP, unsubscribe, revocation, or other reasonable opt-out requests;
- spoof or obscure sender identity;
- use rotating numbers, domains, accounts, or providers to evade carrier filtering or enforcement;
- send prohibited or restricted content through Azure Communication Services or another provider;
- send marketing messages through the RestOps-360 Security Codes program; or
- fail to comply with applicable carrier, telecommunications, consent, quiet-hour, registration, and campaign requirements.

### 2.5 Content and unlawful activity

- promote or facilitate fraud, phishing, identity theft, violence, exploitation, illegal discrimination, unlawful gambling, trafficking, or other illegal activity;
- distribute hateful, threatening, defamatory, obscene, or unlawfully harassing content;
- infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, confidentiality, or other rights;
- use AI-assisted features to generate or facilitate unlawful or harmful conduct; or
- use the Services in violation of export controls, sanctions, anti-bribery laws, tax laws, employment laws, food-safety laws, or other applicable requirements.

## 3. API and Automation Use

You may use APIs and automation only as documented and authorized. You must implement reasonable rate limiting, credential protection, retry logic, error handling, and access controls.

We may throttle, block, or suspend integrations that create security risk, excessive load, repeated errors, unauthorized access attempts, or material impact to other customers.

## 4. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and may:

- request information or corrective action;
- remove or restrict content or functionality;
- throttle, block, suspend, or terminate access;
- preserve information as required for investigation or law;
- notify affected parties, providers, carriers, payment networks, or authorities where appropriate; and
- take other reasonable steps to protect the Services and others.

When practical and consistent with safety and law, we will provide notice and an opportunity to cure. Immediate action may be taken for fraud, security threats, unlawful messaging, payment abuse, or imminent harm.

## 5. Reporting and Appeals

Report suspected abuse to `contact@mindfultechsol.com` with the subject **Acceptable Use Report** or **Security Report**, as appropriate.

A customer may appeal an enforcement decision by contacting the same address and providing relevant facts. We will review the appeal in good faith, but may maintain restrictions while risk remains.
