# Data Retention Policy

Status: Drafted for legal/operations review.

## General Lifecycle

Sensitive information follows the hierarchy lifecycle unless longer retention is legally required:

1. Active hierarchy.
2. 30-day archive after hierarchy deletion begins.
3. Permanent deletion.

If longer retention is legally required, retain only the minimum required information.

## Sensitive Records

Sensitive records include EIN/SSN, W-9s, banking information, ACH authorizations, electronic signatures, tax documents, vendor tax information, vendor banking details, payment events, and audit logs.

## Legal Exceptions

Retention exceptions may apply for:

- Tax reporting.
- Accounting and financial records.
- ACH/e-sign authorization proof.
- Payment disputes, reversals, returns, and chargebacks.
- Security incidents and fraud prevention.
- Contract and litigation holds.

## Signature Records

Electronic signature records are retained for the duration required by applicable contractual, financial, tax, regulatory, and legal recordkeeping requirements.

## Deletion Documentation

Deletion events must record scope, requester, authorization, deletion executor, archive period, permanent deletion timestamp, and retained exceptions.
